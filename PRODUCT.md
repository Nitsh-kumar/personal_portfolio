# Portfolio product scope

## Audience and purpose

Recruiters and engineering leads evaluating Nitish Kumar for AI engineering roles. Help a visitor understand relevant experience, inspect project evidence, and find a contact channel.

## Current interface

A Next.js single-page portfolio with career information, project cards, skills, visual artwork, a local FAQ widget, and contact links. The FAQ selects predefined replies. The contact form opens an email draft; it does not send email.

## Positioning

AI engineering with Python, RAG, agent workflows, and SAP experience. Keep personal project claims separate from employer work. GPTCraft is a personal chat application, not the employer's CRAFT migration platform.

## Content requirements

- Link projects to inspectable source and state their actual maturity.
- Distinguish implemented, simulated, and planned features.
- Do not claim live deployment, MCP integration, model availability, latency, or production readiness without evidence.
- Keep role, stack, resume, profile, project cards, and FAQ facts consistent.
- Confirm the canonical portfolio domain before setting production metadata.

## Contact and privacy

Use the existing approved contact links. Review public resume and contact details before publishing. Visitors must send the email draft themselves.

## Design and verification

Preserve the current visual design. Use [DESIGN.md](DESIGN.md) as a design specification, not proof of measured contrast or performance. Check keyboard interaction, reduced motion, WebGL fallback, responsive layout, lint, and build before release.

See [project status](docs/PROJECT_STATUS.md) for next milestones. This documentation pass does not verify live hosting or change the UI.
